# Bugfix Requirements Document

## Introduction

This bugfix now governs deterministic visual convergence of the complete Golden Room proof-assembly PNG, not only the recliner. The locked Canon remains immutable appearance/composition evidence; the existing own empty-twin remains immutable room/scene evidence; and every candidate must be a deterministic render of the assembled 3D world under the approved spatial and camera contracts. The current three-panel comparison demonstrates the defect: the proof assembly is sparse, blocky, and materially unlike the locked Canon, so it cannot truthfully claim a 95% visual match.

The completed recliner bug-condition and root-cause requirements remain in force as a critical v2 subcomponent of room convergence. Candidate fingerprint `48f2e5c610f0661419a9a2c70ba5bdbe7511ef70cc0c9b830e3faaebd98ce0e6` passed qwen2.5vl screening but failed closed under primary adjudication because its appearance materially diverges from the locked Canon. Its remediation remains bound to immutable Task 11.8.4b structural baseline fingerprint `d220ae78b3c8fd327a5aeb6aca523fd0ee5b132429c6947b1d413e89f5d204e9`, authoritative `13-art-direction.md` SHA-256 `40dbcb7d0c9d3b0646668f0878ea3994f5b46178ff45909140260988a556e007`, locked Canon SHA-256 `dbbaa35c9aafd64de2735a29da8eea5a1852e08805a5746563f6f2d45100a3b6`, and stable recliner UUID `3b2cae03-3556-5c1e-a19b-ea3c1e15694c` as previously recorded.

Success is fail-closed and hash-bound. It requires a calibrated deterministic 95% contract, fixed render protocol, complete Golden Room inventory and geometry checks, multiple perceptual metrics, strict local vision, independent primary adjudication, deterministic dual replay, relevant build/load/material tests, and explicit human approval of the exact passing hashes. Iteration must be durable and resumable rather than dependent on a chat session: bounded iterations continue through a Windows-owned loop that already exists or explicit bounded invocations until exact success, explicit user interruption, or a hard environmental blocker. Task 11.8.5 and every release or qualification path remain blocked until the full room contract and exact-hash human approval pass.
