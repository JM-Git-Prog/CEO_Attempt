V11 RELATION SEMANTICS — apply these geometric operations before placing anything:
* adjacent_to / east_of: places subject EAST of target (x increases). Never for a south row.
* west_of: places subject WEST of target.
* south_of: places subject SOUTH of target (z decreases). Use for seating rows along a counter's south face.
* north_of: places subject NORTH of target.
* above: places subject directly above target at ceiling. Use for pendant lights hanging above a surface.
* around: distributes subjects in a circle around target with equal spacing.
* centered: places at room center with optional x,z offsets.

V11 OPENING INTENT — apply this placement operation after relationships but before camera:

V11 CAMERA INTENT — apply this placement operation after relationships and openings:
